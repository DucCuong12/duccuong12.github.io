abstract: >-
  Overview: SAVIOR turns long-horizon robotic video generation into the recursive evolution of a verified task state.
  It tracks task progress, checks whether each generated transition advances the manipulation goal while preserving
  previously verified states, and regenerates failed segments before they corrupt the rollout. Recovered actions from
  the verified videos then drive real-world humanoid execution, with gains across open-source and commercial video generators.
